How we work

A holding company, carrying on no regulated activity

Noire UK Holdings Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 14842543, incorporated in May 2023, with its registered office at 13 St. Marys Street, C/O Psf Accounting Ltd, Stamford PE9 2DE.

We hold shares in our subsidiaries and administer the group. We are not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority or the Prudential Regulation Authority, and we carry on no regulated activity: no advising, no arranging, no dealing or managing, no credit or insurance mediation, no deposits and no funds from the public. Where a company in the group does carry on a regulated activity, it does so under its own permissions, in its own name, and is responsible for it.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I invest in the group?

No. We hold our shareholdings on our own behalf, accept no outside money and offer no securities. Nothing on this site is an offer or a financial promotion.

Are you FCA authorised?

No, and we do not need to be, because we carry on no regulated activity. You can check any firm's status on the Financial Services Register at register.fca.org.uk.

Your SIC code says financial services holding company. What does that mean?

It describes a holding company whose subsidiaries operate in that sector. It does not mean the holding company itself provides financial services, and this one does not.

Can you advise me on my investments or pension?

No. That is a regulated activity and we are not authorised to carry it on. Speak to an FCA-authorised adviser.

Do you provide services to other companies?

No. These functions exist for our own subsidiaries. We are not a company secretarial, accountancy or consultancy provider for third parties.

How do I verify the company?

Search company number 14842543 on the Companies House register at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk.

Need to verify the company?

Send an enquiry and we will confirm the registered details and the right contact.
